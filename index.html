Explore Głogów

✦

Making a trip of it? Głogów is one of Silesia's oldest towns — a thousand years of history on the banks of the Odra, famous for holding out against Emperor Henry V's siege in 1109.

History

Collegiate Church & Cathedral Island

The Gothic collegiate church on Ostrów Tumski — one of the oldest sites in Silesia, rebuilt after WWII. Cross the Odra to reach it.

Map → Museum

Castle of the Dukes of Głogów

Home to the Archaeological & Historical Museum. A perfect rainy-morning plan.

Map → Stroll

Old Town & Town Hall

The market square and rebuilt town hall at the heart of the Old Town — cafés, ice cream and evening walks.

Map → History

St Nicholas Church ruins

Roofless Gothic walls preserved as a permanent ruin. Atmospheric at golden hour.

Map → Culture

Andreas Gryphius Theatre

Historic theatre rebuilt in the Old Town, named after the Głogów-born Baroque poet. Concerts need no translation.

Map → Outdoors

Odra riverbank

Walk along the river between the Old Town and Cathedral Island — the best views of the skyline.

Polish wedding 101

“Gorzko! Gorzko!”
“Bitter!” When guests chant this, the couple must kiss to sweeten the vodka. Join in — loudly.
“Sto lat”
“A hundred years” — the song for every toast and happy moment. You'll know it by the second round.
Pace yourself
Polish receptions are marathons: several hot courses, cake, and dancing well past midnight.
Oczepiny
Games after midnight, often with a veil and bouquet toss. Expect to be pulled onto the dance floor.
